# Legal And Attribution Notes

## Battlecast Reuse Permission

D20bench may reuse Battlecast code, data, and assets because both projects share the same author and copyright holder, and that author has explicitly granted D20bench permission to copy or transform Battlecast material.

The sibling Battlecast checkout at `../battlecast` must remain read-only. Any reused material should be copied, transformed, or generated into this repository.

## Provenance Requirements

Battlecast-derived imports should record:

- Source repository: `https://github.com/opengandalf/battlecast.git`
- Source commit SHA.
- Source file path.
- Import script or manual-copy note.
- Whether the material is SRD-derived content, Battlecast-original code, Battlecast-original UI/assets, or generated benchmark data.

## SRD 5.2 Content

SRD 5.2 content remains licensed under CC-BY-4.0. D20bench should keep attribution metadata with imported SRD-derived monsters, rules text, spells, and other data.

## Hidden Evaluation Suites

D20bench will support visible public scenarios, hidden evaluation suites, and private Elo arena seasons. Public reports may share archived ratings and replay summaries without disclosing hidden scenario details when needed to preserve benchmark integrity.
